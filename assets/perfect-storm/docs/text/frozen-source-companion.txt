1. Governing Freeze Rule
The existing PDFs are strong enough to stand as historical publication artifacts. This 
companion exists so that later clarification does not become retroactive rewriting. The sanitized 
report remains the public-review artifact; the unsanitized reconstruction remains a separately 
labeled reconstructed internal-review artifact. Neither is to be silently 'fixed' in place.
 No overwrite of the frozen PDFs.
 No silent replacement of wording, citations, dates, or provenance.
 No conversion of a later interpretation into a claim that the earlier document always said it.
 No deletion of disagreement, uncertainty labels, or source-lineage notes merely because a later 
version can say them more cleanly.
 Corrections, attribution improvements, and framing clarifications are recorded here or in a 
later numbered companion.
Rule: frozen source + additive companion + preserved diff/ledger.
2. The Scope of Accountability
This is not a report about one company being uniquely reckless. Named organizations appear 
because public incidents, experiments, disclosures, standards work, or research provide evidence 
for specific mechanisms. The target of the warning is the field-level failure structure.
The problem is not OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face, one university, one 
laboratory, or one safety team. The problem is a field building increasingly 
agentic, connected, automated systems across overlapping trust domains while 
still depending on controls that may fail together.
Institutional accountability is not institutional accusation. The paper's demand is that AI safety, 
cybersecurity, biosecurity, laboratory automation, standards bodies, vendors, cloud providers, and 
high-consequence research programs prove that their boundaries are real, independent, tested, and 
able to survive compound failure.
Author-scope statement: The field needs to get its shit together before individually survivable 
weaknesses line up into a compound failure.
3. Perfect Storm Is a Compound-Failure Framework — Not a Single Viromimetic 
Scenario
Core clarification: AI-LC-IEV / Live-Code In Executio Viromimetics is an author-defined conceptual 
branch in the research lineage. It is not the master mechanism of the Perfect Storm report, and the 
report does not require a digital 'virus' to exist for its control-plane thesis to matter.
The broader hypothesis is composable failure across heterogeneous systems. A serious event 
could be produced by different combinations of isolation failure, credential or permission failure, 
shared infrastructure, artifact or package propagation, unintended agent coordination, excessive 
tool authority, automation failure, human procedural error, institutional delay, compromised 
monitoring, weak revocation, or cyber-physical actuation.

not replicate by the same mechanism. They can, however, exhibit comparable systems-level 
patterns: transmission paths, node or host dependence, persistent state, amplification, adaptation, 
hidden bridges, weak barriers, common-mode failure, and cascading consequence.

5. The Machine Clock: Boundary Failure to External Effect
Do not freeze an unsupported stopwatch into the thesis. The report should not claim that every 
escape-to-external-effect chain takes 'under a minute.' The defensible engineering statement is that

once an intended digital boundary fails, subsequent steps may operate on machine timescales, and 
the exact interval depends on architecture.
Useful latency model:
T_total = T_boundary + T_internal_traversal + T_authority + T_model/tool + 
T_external_action
 T_boundary: time required to cross the intended sandbox or isolation boundary.
 T_internal_traversal: time to move through reachable internal trust paths.
 T_authority: time to obtain or reuse credentials, permissions, write paths, or service authority.
 T_model/tool: inference, orchestration, planning, or tool-call latency.
 T_external_action: time for the external service, registry, device, model, or endpoint to accept 
and execute the action.
Human comparison clock: detection + comprehension + escalation + authority + coordination + 
intervention. If the machine traverses the failure graph before the human response loop can 
establish what happened and who can stop it, the architecture is already behind.
6. Fan-Out Is More Important Than Model Count
One endpoint can be a junction between many systems. A phone, workstation, service account, 
browser session, or orchestration host may simultaneously connect to multiple LLMs, cloud 
accounts, repositories, messaging systems, local files, APIs, package registries, or automation tools.
The important question is not 'How many AI systems are there?' It is: How many supposedly 
separate systems can one compromised identity, device, artifact, or control path reach?
This is a trust-graph problem. Physical separation is not sufficient evidence of independence when 
systems share identity, packages, cloud control planes, secrets, vendors, writable integration layers, 
or remote interfaces.
7. Polymorphism, Persistence, and the False All-Clear
Adaptive or polymorphic behavior compresses the useful recognition window. It can make 
signature-based identification less dependable because the same underlying failure process may 
not continue to present in the same form.
Containment is not the same as eradication. Killing the visible process does not prove the 
incident is over if another branch retains authority, persistence, credentials, writable state, a 
trusted artifact, a backup path, a remote session, or another route back into the system.
 Revoke compromised authority, not merely the first process observed.
 Sever reachable trust paths and rotate or invalidate credentials.
 Re-establish trusted provenance for artifacts, packages, models, and configurations.
 Verify independent interlocks and monitoring from a trust root not controlled by the 
compromised environment.
 Do not declare an all-clear until persistence and re-entry paths have been tested.

8. What the Report Is Actually Asking People to Test
The test target is not one monster scenario. The test target is the composition of boundaries, 
clocks, trust paths, and independent safeguards.
 Can an evaluation or sandbox environment produce unintended external effects despite being 
described as isolated?
 Can one identity, artifact, package, service, or orchestration layer bridge environments that are 
assumed to be independent?
 How much fan-out exists from one endpoint or service account into other models, tools, cloud 
services, repositories, or control systems?
 Do artifact-promotion and software-supply-chain paths remain writable from lower-trust 
environments?
 Can autonomous or semi-autonomous systems act faster than meaningful human review and 
intervention?
 Do supposedly independent safeguards share the same cloud, identity, model family, key 
material, logging plane, or orchestration dependency?
 Can a digital compromise reach scientific or industrial hardware, and if so, is final safety 
enforcement deterministic and outside model authority?
 After containment, what proves that no surviving branch, credential, state, or artifact can re-
enter?
 What evidence would genuinely falsify or reduce the concern: hardware-enforced isolation, 
broken trust bridges, independently tested deterministic interlocks, mature cyberbiosecurity 
standards, adversarial validation, and transparent near-miss reporting?
9. Evidence Boundary
This companion tightens interpretation without upgrading inference into evidence. The 
existing report's core discipline remains controlling: constituent mechanisms have public support 
at different evidence grades; the complete end-to-end biological catastrophe remains unobserved in 
public evidence; and a numerical full-chain probability is not established.
 Observed components are not erased because the whole chain has not occurred.
 Observed components do not become proof that the full chain is likely.
 Unknown is not proof of danger.
 Unknown is also not evidence of safety.
 Where a bridge is demonstrably broken and independent controls survive realistic adversarial 
testing, concern should decrease.
Feasibility framing, not inevitability. Consequence-driven engineering, not invented probability.

10. Citation and Attribution Supplement — Without Rewriting the PDFs
The frozen PDFs should not be reopened merely to improve bibliography formatting. Use this 
companion and the GitHub page to provide citation hygiene, contributor credit, and any later 
source additions.
 For event claims, prefer the first-party incident source and pair it with an independent review 
when one exists.
 Where a paper has named authors, credit the authors rather than citing only the institution 
when practical.
 Where a collaboration materially contributed to a capability demonstration, credit the 
collaborating institution as well as the publisher.
 Keep direct sources from affected parties when they provide distinct forensic or technical 
perspective; do not let a single organization's narrative stand in for the entire event.
 Historical NASA/CSB/NIST/FAA material supports human-failure classes and organizational 
mechanisms; it does not prove the AI/bio cascade.
 Author-defined concepts such as CPX and HDA-100 should remain explicitly author-defined. 
External literature supports analogous mechanisms, not authorship of those frameworks.
Citation-supplement rule: If a new primary source or corrected attribution is discovered after 
freeze, append it to the GitHub citation supplement with a date and reason. Do not alter the 
historical PDF merely to make the reference list look newer.

13. Canonical One-Paragraph Interpretation
Perfect Storm at the Control Plane is a compound-failure systems framework. It 
examines how different digital, human, institutional, and cyber-physical failure 
mechanisms can become coupled through shared trust and control planes, 
especially when machine-speed action outruns human-speed containment. AI-LC-
IEV / viromimetics is one author-defined conceptual branch in that lineage, not 
the required mechanism of the report. The complete catastrophic chain has not 
been publicly observed; the purpose is to test whether the bridges and safeguards 
that would have to stop it are genuinely independent, deterministic, and 
survivable under compound failure.
