# Gate Closing / AI Pincer — Prospective Failure + Resurrection Protocol

## Purpose

This addendum fixes the scoring rules before future Gate-Closing receipts arrive. It does not rewrite the August 13 prediction. The original prediction, prior contradictions, ashes, revisions, and receipts remain visible.

## Pressure-Release Prediction

Gate pressure will not necessarily tighten monotonically. Major governed platforms may temporarily loosen restrictions, expand access, reopen a lane, or reduce friction. Where a meaningful release occurs, the forward prediction is that at least some major governed platforms will later restore or replace that pressure with a stronger, broader, more consequential, or differently positioned gate.

A temporary loosening is **not** scored as support for this prediction. At the moment of loosening it is recorded as a real contradiction, release, or counter-receipt and placed into **ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE** for the second stage.

## Failure Condition

Failure is real. The relevant claim loses confidence, becomes **PARTIAL FAIL**, **CONTRADICTORY**, or **ASH** when the expected mechanism does not recur within its stated window, when a durable opposite pattern dominates, or when a predicted second-stage tightening never follows a pressure release.

A failed claim remains in the historical record and cannot be silently reworded after the fact.

## Resurrection Condition

Resurrection is also allowed, but only prospectively and visibly. If later evidence directly restores a previously failed mechanism—especially a sequence of pressure release followed by a stronger or more consequential gate—the claim may be reopened as **RESURRECTED**, **REOPENED**, or **ASH → DESCENDANT**.

Resurrection does not erase the contradiction that came first. Receipt A and Receipt B are both retained. The original claim and any revised descendant are scored separately so the ledger shows the entire path rather than retroactively converting every miss into a hit.

## Two-Receipt Rule

**RECEIPT A — CONTRADICTION / PRESSURE RELEASE:** A platform materially loosens, reopens, expands, or neutralizes a gate. This receipt counts against any claim of continuous one-way tightening.

**ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE — WAITING:** No victory is claimed from the release itself. The sequence remains open.

**RECEIPT B — RESURRECTION / STRONGER GATE:** A later event restores the mechanism through stronger licensing, provenance, identity, volume, discovery, export, monetization, ranking, or distribution control. Only then is the release→re-tightening sequence scored as supportive of the pressure-release prediction.

## Cadence Clarification

The earlier roughly six-month timing estimate is not load-bearing. It was a rough interval estimate, not the core mechanism. The surviving cadence claim is that Gate-Closing events recur. Their actual timing may be irregular, clustered, trigger-driven, or market-specific. The interval estimate can fail while the recurrence claim survives.

## Prospective Scoring States

Future receipts will be labeled **SUPPORTIVE**, **MIXED**, **CONTRADICTORY**, **UNRELATED**, **ACCOUNTS RECEIVABLE**, **PARTIAL FAIL**, **ASH**, **RESURRECTED**, **REOPENED**, or **ASH → DESCENDANT**.

No later event may alter the text or date of an earlier frozen prediction.

## Anti-Post-Hoc Statement

This failure-and-resurrection protocol was written and frozen before the future release→re-tightening events it is intended to score. Its purpose is to prevent both kinds of hindsight abuse: declaring every contradiction fatal before the predicted second stage can occur, and declaring every contradiction secretly supportive after a later reversal. **Both receipts stay on the books.**
