Identity

Full Spectrum Independent

Not red. Not blue. Not green. The point is not to average parties into beige mush. It is to carry useful truths from competing political traditions without belonging to any of them.

Red · responsibility / consequence
Green · independent / outside pressure
Blue · access / mercy / public investment
What this site is

A front door, not a brochure.

The landing page identifies the candidate and the architecture. The deeper tabs carry the actual machinery: policy papers, Explorers, evidence, Gate Closing, source integrity, the Presidential Address, voting-rights information, research surfaces, and receipts. Each surface remains distinct so the site can grow additively instead of collapsing into one giant scroll.

Dual-Partisan // The Weld

You’re not blending left and right into mush.

You’re forcing opposing truths to coexist in one structure. Compassion without accountability fails. Accountability without compassion fails. So weld them together and make the system carry both.
“You’re both carrying something true. Get in the damn corner until the design can carry both.”
This doctrine is carried into the Presidential Address; the Address tab will get the deeper timestamped and BSL-linked treatment when that tab is built.
