Source Integrity.

The site is allowed to be loud. The evidence is not allowed to cheat. Raw artifacts stay raw, derivatives stay labeled, missing evidence stays missing, corrections become new lineage, and a claim does not become stronger because several models liked it.

Rule 01Preserve the source.

Raw files do not get overwritten by presentation edits.

Rule 02Label the derivative.

Charts, decks, model syntheses and reconstructions stay downstream.

Rule 03Show the wound.

Corrections, contradictions and failed claims stay visible.

Rule 04Receipt before promotion.

A hash proves file integrity, not truth.

Federate, do not fuse

One claim, seven states.

1 · RAW

Original file, capture, source page, dataset, transcript or first-party record.

2 · EXTRACT

Verbatim facts and metadata pulled without interpretation.

3 · DERIVE

Calculation, chart or transformation with formula visible.

4 · SYNTHESIZE

Interpretation stays separate from primary evidence.

5 · ATTACK

Counter-source, alternative explanation, denominator check.

6 · ADJUDICATE

Supported, narrowed, open, rejected or superseded with reasons.

7 · RECEIPT

Version, hash, source list, limitations, gaps and next gate.

Lineage remains inspectable.

Hashes are not truth certificates
A SHA-256 receipt can show a file did not change. It cannot establish that the claim is accurate, the source primary, the calculation valid or the inference justified.
Model agreement is not independent confirmation
Models can share training data, retrieval sources, prompt framing and mistaken premises. Convergence tells us where to inspect; it does not upgrade weak evidence.

Site doctrine

Underpromise. Overbuild. Fix forever.

The private master can be more complete, speculative and reviewer-facing than the public campaign surface. Public pages are derivatives with claim/source gates, not replacements for the master.

Raw evidence preservation
Raw chats, screenshots, videos, ZIPs, datasets, PDFs and source artifacts remain untouched. Derived crops, charts, posters and summaries never overwrite them.
Missing evidence
Missing raw evidence is marked missing until recovered. A later derivative is not promoted to recovered original.
Publication families
A compendium can be a bundle while each white paper remains its own publication object with separate source review, metadata and final packaging.
Uncomfortable rule

The evidence does not belong to the author.

Authorship creates responsibility, not authority over reality. Credentials do not settle reality either. Inspectability, criticism, correction and appropriate expert review are load-bearing.

Public challenge is a feature.

Blueprint proposals and targets remain subject to evidence, pilot results, correction and public challenge.

The past stays in the record. Not as an excuse. Not as a costume. As provenance.
