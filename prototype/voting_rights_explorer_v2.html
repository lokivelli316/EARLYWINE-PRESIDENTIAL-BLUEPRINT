Federal layer

The rights people forget to ask about.

This is the category map for the federal research pass. It is intentionally written as a research contract rather than a finished legal conclusion. Each category will eventually carry the controlling authority, plain-English scope, exceptions, and an as-of date.

Public-source discipline: Constitution, statutes, regulations, controlling federal guidance, and official state authorities outrank campaign wording. When sources conflict or fact patterns change the result, the page must say so.
Status taxonomy

No beige mush. No fake certainty.

The status language is designed to keep legal scope visible instead of flattening everything into yes/no. The same state may have different statuses across different rights.

RETAINED

Source supports that the right or protection remains, subject to stated exceptions.

LIMITED

A real restriction exists; the interface must state who it applies to and under what authority.

STATE-DEPENDENT

Federal baseline does not answer the full question. State law controls a material part of the result.

RESTORABLE

A legal mechanism may restore the right, but the page must not assume the visitor qualifies.

VERIFY / OPEN

Source conflict, missing authority, unresolved fact pattern, or data not yet audited. Never auto-promote.
