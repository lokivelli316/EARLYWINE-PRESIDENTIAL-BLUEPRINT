Concept Attribution — Financial Feedback Exploitation / Circular Feedback Bear Train

This branch of the Perfect Storm framework began while Michael Davis was listening to the Perfect Storm material and asked Robert Michael Earlywine-Lucas (Lokivelli), “Well, what about stocks?” That question opened the branch of inquiry that became the Financial Feedback Exploitation / Circular Feedback Bear Train model. Earlywine-Lucas subsequently developed the question into the formal systems-risk model and integrated it into the broader Perfect Storm framework.

Conceptual-origin credit for this specific ring is shared equally: Michael Davis — 50%; Robert Michael Earlywine-Lucas — 50%.

This attribution applies specifically to the Financial Feedback Exploitation / Circular Feedback Bear Train concept and does not imply co-originatorship or co-authorship of the broader Perfect Storm framework.
