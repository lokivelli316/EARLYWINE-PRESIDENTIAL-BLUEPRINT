Pressure-Release Prediction

Gate pressure is not required to tighten monotonically. A major governed platform may loosen a restriction, reopen a lane, expand access, or reduce friction. Where a meaningful release occurs, the forward prediction is that at least some major governed platforms will later restore or replace that pressure with a stronger, broader, more consequential, or differently positioned gate.

Failure Condition

Failure is real. If the expected mechanism does not recur within its stated window, a durable opposite pattern dominates, or a predicted second-stage tightening never follows a pressure release, the relevant claim is scored CONTRADICTORY, PARTIAL FAIL, or ASH. The original failed wording remains visible.

Resurrection Condition

Resurrection is allowed only visibly and prospectively. If later evidence directly restores a previously failed mechanism — especially a pressure release followed by a stronger or more consequential gate — the claim may be reopened as RESURRECTED, REOPENED, or ASH → DESCENDANT. The earlier contradiction is not deleted, and the original and revised claim are scored separately.

Cadence Clarification

The earlier roughly six-month interval was a rough estimate, not the load-bearing claim. The surviving cadence claim is recurrence: Gate-Closing events are expected to recur, while their actual timing may be irregular, clustered, trigger-driven, or market-specific. The interval estimate can fail while the recurrence claim survives.

Anti-Post-Hoc Rule

No later event may alter the text or date of an earlier frozen prediction. Revisions and descendants receive new dates and separate scores. Both the contradiction receipt and any later resurrection receipt stay on the books.
