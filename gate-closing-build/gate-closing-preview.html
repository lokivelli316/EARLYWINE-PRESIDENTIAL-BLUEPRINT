Gate
Closing.

A public evidence instrument for tracking where access becomes more expensive, permission-dependent, subscription-dependent, platform-dependent, less portable, or less economically viable — while preserving the places where the gate is actually opening.

Current bounded thesis: Gate Closing survives as identifiable mechanisms, not a universal law. The strongest pattern is in governed hosted creation, distribution, monetization, API, and publication lanes. Open technical and self-hosted alternatives remain materially real in several markets.

Open technical alternatives

Open-weight and self-hosted generation remain live counter-evidence. A governed hosted lane is not the same thing as universal loss of technical capability.

Music

Spotify anti-fraud rationale

The 1,000-stream threshold can be read as a creator gate or as protection against bot-farming and noise exploitation. Intentional anti-creator framing remains unproven.

Cross-cutting

Method

Agreement is not proof

Multi-LLM convergence can identify where to inspect. It does not upgrade weak sourcing, remove contradictions, or convert estimates into facts.

Do not publish as fact yet

Open / vulnerable claims

Discovery Mode suppresses non-participants
The audit verifies the commission mechanism and participant uplift claims. It does not establish causal organic-reach punishment for tracks that do not opt in.
Nelson Memo will enrich commercial publishers
The zero-embargo mandate is real. The downstream business-model outcome remains open because Diamond OA and transformative agreements can pull in the opposite direction.
KDP Select is an illegal monopsony
Exclusivity is verified. A competition-law conclusion requires market-share and causal foreclosure evidence that the current corpus does not contain.
X API exact 2026 meter rates
The audit contains exact rates but also identifies its reliance on secondary developer sources as a source-integrity weakness. Keep the numbers visible with that warning until primary documentation is captured.

What this tab refuses to do
No universal Gate Closing index. No arbitrary probability meter. No fake six-month cadence. No claim that every paid service is closed. No causal participation decline without longitudinal evidence.
Gate Closing survives only where the evidence survives. This public tab is an audit instrument, not a thesis-protection machine.
