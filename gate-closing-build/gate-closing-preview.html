Failure condition

The prediction is allowed to lose.

If the expected mechanism fails to recur within its stated window, a durable opposite pattern dominates, or the predicted second-stage tightening never arrives, score the relevant claim CONTRADICTORY, PARTIAL FAIL or ASH. Keep the historical miss visible.

Resurrection condition

The prediction is allowed to come back.

If later evidence directly restores a previously failed mechanism — especially pressure release followed by a stronger or more consequential gate — reopen it as RESURRECTED, REOPENED or ASH → DESCENDANT. Do not erase the contradiction that came first.

Closing rule

Prediction above. Receipts below.

The prophecy is allowed to be theatrical. The audit is not. Predictions are allowed to fail, ashes are allowed to stay ash, descendants are allowed to survive, and a later reversal is allowed to resurrect a mechanism only when the second receipt actually arrives.

Gate Closing survives only where the evidence survives. This public sub-tab is an audit instrument with a frozen prediction layer, not a thesis-protection machine.
